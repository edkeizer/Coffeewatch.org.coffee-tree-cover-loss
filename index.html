About these figures
Tree cover loss
Area of tree cover lost per year, in hectares, where the canopy density was 30–100%, inside the potential coffee area.
Potential coffee area threshold
The FDaP Coffee (2026a, year 2024) probability cut-off used to outline the potential coffee area. 0.28 outlines a wider area than 0.33; the workbook's Countries summary uses 0.33.
Canopy density classes
Over 70%, 60–70%, 40–60% and 30–40%; together they make up the 30–100% total.
Sources
Tree cover loss: Hansen et al., University of Maryland (UMD) Global Forest Change. Potential coffee area: FDaP Coffee model, Forest Data Partnership. Data processing: Google Earth Engine.
Maps
White shows the potential coffee area. Yellow to dark red shows the year of tree cover loss from 2002 to 2025; darker means more recent.
Left out
Costa Rica and Guatemala have figures only at the 0.33 threshold. Guatemala's sheet holds the 0.33 table twice; it is counted once.
